# Validation against the scripts and their results

This file records how `piperom` was checked against the scripts and stored results in
`code implementation for paper/`, as corrected in commit c13ed81 (2026-10-06) and on 2026-10-09 ([legacy_issues.md](legacy_issues.md), E5–E6), and what matches. Results are
regenerated by the scripts in `validation/`; the tables below summarise them. Every comparison uses the
engine's **default settings**.

| Part of the engine | Reference | Sample | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Archetype inputs](#1-archetype-inputs) | arguments of `Pushover2D/CS_Lumped_Iter_<tag>_cont_PO.py` | 18 archetypes | **identical** |
| [Pseudo-pushover](#2-equivalent-static-procedure-and-pseudo-pushover) | `Pushover2D/pushover_results_*.txt` | 18 archetypes × 50 Δc, every stored value | **identical** (3 decimals) |
| [SDOF parameters](#3-equivalent-sdof) | `Pushover_SDOF/sdof_from_2d.py`; `code_proposed_procedure/` | 18 archetypes + the proposed procedure | **identical** |
| [SDOF pushover](#4-sdof-pushover) | `Pushover_SDOF/Results<tag>_SDOF/DispC.out`, `VbaseC.out` | 18 archetypes × 1000 steps | **identical** (6 digits) |
| [SDOF time history](#5-sdof-time-history) | `Pushover_SDOF/Results<tag>_SDOF/*_NLTHA_peak_displacements.csv`; `code_proposed_procedure/Results/NLTHA/` | all 7920 archetype runs (44 records × IM1–IM10) + 150 | **identical** (4 decimals), completed and collapse flags included |
| [3D verification](#6-3d-verification) | `Results/<M>_biron_DispX/Y.txt` | 9 models × 1 ground motion × 2 orientations at IM10 | **identical** (2 decimals), collapses included |

## How to reproduce

| Command | Covers | Output | Time* |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pytest` | inputs, regressions (§1–4), time histories (samples of §5–6), app | — | ~5 min |
| `python validation/run_validation.py` | §1–3 | `validation/report.md` | ~2 min |
| `python validation/run_timehistory_validation.py [--only sdof\|procedure\|3d]` | §5–6 | `validation/timehistory_report.md` | SDOF ~35 min, procedure ~2 min, 3D ~10 min (20 cores) |

\* On a 22-core machine. The run times written into the reports depend on machine load.

Helpers in `validation/legacy.py`:
- **Script readers:** the drivers' arguments (`driver_inputs`, executing a driver with a stub in place of
  the analysis), the scripts' SDOF (`script_sdof`, `script_sdof_model`) and stored results
  (`script_sdof_peaks`, `script_3d_peak`, `procedure_*`).
- **`capture_opensees_calls`:** runs a script with a recording stand-in for OpenSees. `convert_3d_models.py`
  uses it to extract the 3D models without executing any analysis.

## 1. Archetype inputs

`inputs/archetypes/<tag>.yaml` were regenerated from the drivers (c13ed81 and the corrections of 2026-10-09; brace masks, branches,
`n_pipes` and the lumped mains had changed). `validation.legacy.compare_inputs` checks length, pipes,
lumped mains and `x_center`, pipe section, hanger grid, brace mask, branches and branch participation
against the driver's arguments: **no difference for any archetype**.

## 2. Equivalent static procedure and pseudo-pushover

**Method.** `piperom` runs each archetype file with the default settings, which equal the drivers'.
Every value of the result file is compared after formatting the engine's value with 3 decimals, as the
files were written:
- Δc, Γ, M_eff, V_b, mass ratio, u_SDOF;
- for every DOF: normalised shape, scaled shape and load pattern (in the drivers' column order, sorted
  by x).

**Result.** All **48,600 values** of the 18 files are identical; the largest raw difference is 5.0e-4,
the rounding of the stored values. This covers the lumped mains (M29x–M31x: 6 mains left of the centre
and 9 right; M61x–M63x: 2), the massless node at `x_center`, the consistent branch split and the base
shear as support reactions.

`tests/test_static_model.py` also checks that, with the consistent split, the applied loads equal the
spring forces of the analysed main, and that the base shear equals the support reactions × the mains on
each side, for every archetype.

## 3. Equivalent SDOF

**Archetypes.** `derive_sdof` with the default settings (Δc = the pushover step closest to 12 mm, i.e.
12.32 mm; values rounded to 3 decimals as written in the pushover results) against
`load_sdof_params(tag, 12.0)`: Δc, Γ, M_eff, and φ and scale of every spring are **identical for all 18
archetypes**. The scales include the lumped mains (6 or 9 per transverse spring of M29x–M31x, 2 for
M61x–M63x) and α·`n_braces` per branch.

**Proposed procedure.** `code_proposed_procedure/equivalent_static.py` stores its result at full
precision (`Results/EquivStatic/equiv_static_nT4_nL3.npz`). `derive_sdof` on
`inputs/examples/equivalent_static_example.yaml` (braces given as `count: 4`) at exactly Δc = 12 mm:

| Quantity | Relative difference |
|---|---|
| brace placement (`compute_stiff_mask`) | identical mask |
| Γ, effective mass, u_SDOF | 0 (full precision) |
| full displaced shape, support shape | 0 |
| scaled Pinching4 envelope of every spring | < 1e-12 |
| the SDOF `NLTHA_SDOF.py` analysed (`sdof_params.json`, rounded) | identical |

## 4. SDOF pushover

`timehistory.run_sdof_pushover` on each archetype's SDOF against the stored output of
`Pushover_SDOF/<tag>_SDOF.py` (monotonic, 1000 steps of 0.05 mm, springs without caps): displacements
and support reactions are **identical to the 6 significant digits stored for all 18 archetypes**
(M61y's files were stale until 2026-10-10, [legacy_issues.md](legacy_issues.md) E4). The cyclic files
(`*_CPO.out`) aren't compared: they're older than the current SDOFs.

## 5. SDOF time history

**Archetypes.** Every record of `S4_IM` at IM1–IM10 (44 × 10 = 440 runs per archetype, 7920 in total), floor 4,
against `Pushover_SDOF/Results<tag>_SDOF/<tag>_NLTHA_peak_displacements.csv`: peak |u| (4 decimals), and
whether the run completed or stopped at collapse. Two variants:
- the engine's time history on the SDOF the script analyses (`script_sdof_model`);
- the full engine chain (`derive_sdof` → `SDOFModel.from_parameters`).

**Result.** Both variants give **identical peaks and flags for all 7920 runs**, including the 446
collapses (179 in M03x).

**Proposed procedure.** The 150 records of `S4_150` against `Results/NLTHA/SDOF_nT4_nL3/Disp_GM<k>_Floor4.txt`
(abs. max of the displacement envelope): **identical for all 150**.

**Why the rounding matters.** The scripts read Γ, M_eff and φ with 3 decimals. With full-precision values
(`sdof.round_decimals: null`) most peaks change slightly, but a few records change by up to ~14%: the
hysteretic response can switch path within the rounding interval (M01x, IM9, record 120522: 23.86 mm with
Γ = 0.90185, 20.94 mm with Γ = 0.902; ±0.01% around either value changes the peak by < 0.01%). The
completed and collapse flags don't change ([legacy_issues.md](legacy_issues.md), E3).

## 6. 3D verification

**Method.** The 3D models in `inputs/models3d/` were extracted from `3D_models/<M>_biron.py` by recording
their OpenSees commands; nothing in the models was changed. The fallback test types of each script are
stored with the model. Each model is analysed by `piperom.verification3d` with the default settings at
IM10, under the first ground motion (records 120111 / 120112) in both orientations.

The compared quantity is the scripts': the largest peak displacement over the braced nodes, per
direction, stored with 2 decimals; 100 = collapse (a braced node exceeded 60 mm and the run stopped).

## What isn't validated

- **SDOF cyclic pushover** (`Pushover_SDOF/Results*/*_CPO.out`): the engine runs it (`piperom sdof
  --cyclic`), but the stored files predate the current SDOFs.
- **3D runs beyond the sample**: one ground motion per model at IM10. The other 43 × 10 runs per model
  follow the same code path.
- **`Section2/`** (demonstration model) and **the notebooks** in `Results/`: not part of the engine.
- The original code before c13ed81 (git history): `branch_split: legacy` still reproduces its branch
  split, but its results are no longer in the repository.
